HunyuanVideo responds best to one paragraph of 60 to 120 words in the present tense. Open with a short summary of the subject and what it does, then the setting, shot type and camera movement, lighting and atmosphere, and state the style at the end. Be literal and visual. For image-to-video, describe only the action and, if it moves, the camera; never restate the lighting or what the image already shows. Avoid lists and on-screen text.
