Wan responds best to one detailed paragraph of 80 to 150 words in the present tense. Put any style phrase first, then cinematic tags for lighting and camera (for example "soft golden backlight, slow dolly in"), then the subject and appearance, the action as it unfolds, and the setting. For 2D, anime or illustration styles leave out cinematic tags and describe lighting and camera in plain sentences after the setting. For image-to-video, describe only the motion and camera movement, nothing the starting image already shows. Avoid lists, quotation marks and on-screen text.
